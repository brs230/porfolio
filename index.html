About

Driven by leadership, service, and engineering ambition.

I am Brenton Schneider, a student at Lehigh University studying to become an engineer. My academic path is paired with real-world leadership experience, including serving as captain of both my soccer and tennis teams in high school. I have learned how to motivate teams, lead under pressure, and stay focused on a common goal.

I also earned recognition at the DECA National Convention through the ARISE program after winning the Massachusetts Quick Pitch activity. In addition, I am an Eagle Scout and led the creation of the Pond Street Recreational Fitness Trail in Norfolk, Massachusetts. Born on May 16, 2008, I am proud to come from Norfolk and to bring both community-minded values and strong leadership into everything I do.
